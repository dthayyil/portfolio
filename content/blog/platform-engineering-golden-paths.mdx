Every team reinventing CI/CD, environments and observability is a tax on your
whole organisation. Platform engineering exists to pay that tax once.

## Golden paths, not golden cages

A **golden path** is the supported, paved route to production: scaffolding, CI/CD,
secrets, observability and rollback — all wired up by default. Crucially it's
*opt-in-by-default*, not mandatory. Teams can step off the path when they must,
but rarely want to.

## Reduce cognitive load

The metric that matters most is **cognitive load**. Every decision a stream-aligned
team doesn't have to make is capacity returned to product work.

- Self-service environments in minutes, not tickets.
- Sensible defaults for Kubernetes, Helm and GitOps.
- Observability and SLOs baked in, not bolted on.

## The payoff

On a recent healthcare modernization, paving the golden path contributed to a
**30% improvement in deployment speed** and materially lower onboarding time — the
platform became the fastest way to ship, so teams adopted it willingly.

Platform engineering is product management for your developers. Treat the platform
as a product, and the adoption takes care of itself.
