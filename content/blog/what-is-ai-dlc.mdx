The Software Development Lifecycle we inherited assumes humans do all the thinking
and machines do the running. That assumption is breaking. When an AI can draft a
design, write a test, review a diff and propose a rollback, the lifecycle itself
needs to change.

## From SDLC to AI-DLC

The **AI-Driven Development Lifecycle (AI-DLC)** keeps the classic phases —
plan, build, test, release, operate — but treats AI as a first-class participant
with *guardrails*, not a bolt-on autocomplete.

- **Plan** — AI drafts options; humans decide.
- **Build** — Copilot accelerates; standards are enforced automatically.
- **Test** — agents generate and triage; coverage becomes a conversation.
- **Release** — risk is scored and gated as policy-as-code.
- **Operate** — agents watch signals and propose actions.

## Governance is the unlock

The organisations winning with AI aren't the ones with the most prompts — they're
the ones with the clearest **guardrails**. Policy-as-code, measurable adoption and
human-in-the-loop review are what let you move fast *safely*.

> AI without governance is velocity without brakes.

The rest of this series digs into each phase — starting with governed GitHub
Copilot enablement.
